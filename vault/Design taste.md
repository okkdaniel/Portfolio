# Design taste

What Daniel has liked, rejected, and asked for. The redesign should feel like **art about how he sees himself, not a tech demo**. See [[Home]], [[Rules]].

## Likes

- **Flat warm paper** (`#f3f0e8`), dark ink text, Cormorant Garamond italic for the name, JetBrains Mono for everything small.
- **Halftone watercolor**: layered multi-opacity dot washes plus confetti dots, like franceszhao.ca. Overlaps darken like pigment.
- **Real PNW nature**, abstract but recognizable: ferns and fiddleheads, moss with spore capsules, lichen, firs, drooping cedar, runners.
- **The frog as negative space**, never as a drawn logo or mascot.
- **The 12fps stop-motion beat** for growth, with ease-out (fast start, slowing finish).
- Restraint: one object per view, no duplication (he disliked the 3D viewer and the render of the same object stacked together).
- Objects sitting straight on the paper (cut-out renders), not on white cards.

## Rejected

- Gradient, fog, vignette, glow, film-grain backgrounds ("feels like vibeslop").
- An animated mascot (the hopping anura frog: "not really my thing").
- Literal circuit-board traces; the dark circuit-forest version (first redesign attempt).
- Images on white/print cards.
- A halftone "dissolve" edge on the 3D model (he asked for it, then said scrap it: the organic outline still read as an edge). Zoom is simply off instead. See [[Project sheets]].

## Copy and the tagline

- Plain, factual copy (see [[Rules]]).
- Tagline history: "builds robots, thinks about trees." was kept for a while, then dropped on 2026-10-04 as corny. The header is **name only** for now; he may add a line later.
- What he wants in a tagline if one comes back: **silly/playful beats earnest**, but **no "builds robots"**, and **no comma-joined two-part line** (that shape itself reads corny to him). The frog idea ("befriends frogs") was the closest he liked. Things he turned down: "Robots, CAD, the outdoors.", "Engineering student. Robotics and CAD.", "I design robots and spend a lot of time outside.", "Mechanical design and robotics, Nevada.", "builds robots, hugs trees / collects pinecones / touches grass."

## Video (frog reveal)

Settled look in [[Frog reveal video]]: 12fps, site-style ease-out (frog clear by ~0.5s), growth ~4.1s, mostly the site's own plants, concentrated around the frog and connected like a vine, few flowers, no trees framing it, many different seeded versions.
- Figure interactions: one gesture, one meaning. Hover = do the thing, pointer = gentle tilt. Pointer-position-to-state mappings (distance from centre etc.) felt confusing (2026-10-07). Figures must stay inside their own space; nothing may grow over text or other previews.
- Figures are sized by eye, not only by maths: a figure can read too big at its computed size (Rival, 2026-10-07), so each kind may carry an optical `scale`.
- A part with no mechanism is best looked over, turntable-style, rather than transformed; four tries at unfolding the stand on hover never sat right (2026-10-07).
- An object should stay grounded: move the view round it rather than turning the object to face you (2026-10-07, the stand: turning it face up read as awkward; unfolding in place under a rising view read right).
- Motion that changes shape should run in readable steps (one thing, then the next), at a steady pace with each step eased, not everything at once on a spring (2026-10-07, the stand's unfold).
